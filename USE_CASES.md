# Use Cases — Contract Renewal Radar

[Live app](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/) · [Plans](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/plans/) · [Discovery call](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/)

## 1. SaaS stack rationalization
An IT team discovers 11 auto-renewing SaaS contracts; 4 are cancelled inside the notice window, saving $86k/year.

## 2. Fleet lease management
Vehicle lease renewals are tracked with mileage terms; renewals align with fuel-cost data from [Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/).

## 3. Vendor renegotiation prep
Before a key MRO supplier renewal, benchmarks from [Spare Price Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/spare-price-scout/) arm procurement for a 14% price reduction.

## 4. Escalation-clause audit
AI flags contracts with CPI+ escalation clauses exceeding current inflation, prioritized for renegotiation.

## 5. Customer renewals
Sales gets 90-day alerts on expiring customer contracts with usage summaries — churn risk addressed before notice windows close.
