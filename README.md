## What it does

Contract Renewal Radar reads your contracts, extracts renewal dates, notice windows, and auto-renewal clauses, then alerts your team in time to renegotiate — so no contract silently renews on bad terms again.

## Key features

- **Clause extraction** — renewal dates, notice periods, price-escalation clauses found by AI.
- **Notice-window alerts** — reminders at 90/60/30 days before cancellation deadlines.
- **Spend dashboard** — total contract value renewing per quarter, by vendor.
- **Renegotiation briefs** — usage and pricing benchmarks summarized before each renewal.
- **Auto-renewal guard** — flags contracts that will lock in without action.

## Who it's for

Procurement, legal ops, and finance teams managing 50+ vendor or customer contracts.
